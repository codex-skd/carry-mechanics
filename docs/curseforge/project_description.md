<h1 align="center">Carry Mechanics</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Pick up, carry, and place blocks and entities with your bare hands!</strong></p>

<hr>

<h2>Overview</h2>

<p>
Carry Mechanics lets you pick up, carry, and place blocks (with their tile entities) and entities using only your empty hands.
Inspired by the classic Carry On mod, rewritten from scratch for modern NeoForge.
</p>

<br>

<h2>Features</h2>

<h3>Pick up anything</h3>
<p>Grab blocks with tile entities (chests, furnaces, spawners, etc.) and living entities (mobs, animals, players) by holding <strong>Shift</strong> + right-clicking with empty hands.</p>

<h3>Place anywhere</h3>
<p>Right-click on any surface to place what you&#8217;re carrying.</p>

<h3>Entity stacking</h3>
<p>Stack carried entities on top of others like a totem pole.</p>

<h3>Weight system</h3>
<p>Heavier objects slow you down more. You cannot jump while carrying. The weight depends on the object size and NBT data.</p>

<h3>Fully configurable</h3>
<p>25+ options including max distance, entity size limits, slowness multipliers, whitelist/blacklist toggles, hit prevention, player pickup toggle, and more.</p>

<h3>Whitelist / Blacklist</h3>
<p>Control what can be picked up via datapack tags for blocks, entities, and stacking (6 tag types).</p>

<h3>Scripting system</h3>
<p>Datapack-powered JSON scripts with conditions (gamestage, advancement, XP, gamemode) and custom commands on init/loop/place.</p>

<h3>Commands</h3>
<p><code>/carrymechanics debug</code> &#8212; Shows current carry state<br>
<code>/carrymechanics clear [player]</code> &#8212; Clears carry data<br>
<code>/carrymechanics place [player]</code> &#8212; Places carried object</p>

<br>

<h2>Controls</h2>

<table>
<tr><th>Action</th><th>Control</th></tr>

<tr><td>Place</td><td>Right-click on surface</td></tr>

</table>
